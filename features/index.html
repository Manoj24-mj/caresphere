THE FOUNDATION

PROTOTYPE BOUNDARIES

Clear, safe and honest in every demo

  • All availability, quantities, distances and update times shown here are illustrative sample data, not live information.
  • The ambulance workflow is a local preview only; it does not dispatch a vehicle or contact emergency services. For a real emergency, call local emergency services.
  • Medicine listings are not treatment advice or a recommendation to substitute a prescription.
  • Scheme eligibility and applications must be verified through official programme channels; nothing is submitted from this demo.
